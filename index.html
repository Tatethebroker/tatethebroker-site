Umbrella insurance

An extra layer when a claim goes past your limits.

If a lawsuit goes beyond your auto or home liability limits, an umbrella policy picks up the rest. It's usually one of the cheapest ways to protect what you've built.

Request a quote
How it works

It sits on top of your other policies.

Your home and auto liability pay first. The umbrella kicks in after those limits run out.

$1,000,000+ limitsProtects you against othersAdd anytime
Who it's for
Homeowners, landlords, families with teen drivers, and anyone with savings or assets to protect.
What it covers
Injury and property damage claims above your other limits, plus some claims those policies exclude, like libel or slander.
What it needs
Carriers usually require certain liability limits on your home and auto first. I'll line those up for you.
When to add it
Anytime. You can add an umbrella in the middle of a policy term.
